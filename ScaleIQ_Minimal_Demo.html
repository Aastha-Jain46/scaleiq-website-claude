How We Work

What it means to be a ScaleIQ company

For portfolio companies, joining ScaleIQ means keeping what makes you great and gaining everything you never had time to build.

01

Retain your identity

Your brand, your team, your technical culture — all intact. ScaleIQ doesn't absorb companies into a corporate monolith. Oges is Oges; future portfolio companies keep their names and autonomy.

02

Gain shared infrastructure

Business development, finance, and HR functions move to ScaleIQ Operations. Your engineers and domain experts stop context-switching into overhead work and focus entirely on delivery.

03

Access group-wide BD

Your technical sales team works in close coordination with ScaleIQ's central BD function — opening doors to clients and sectors that individual companies struggle to reach alone.

04

Grow with investment backing

ScaleIQ Capital ensures each portfolio company has the runway to invest in product, people, and market development — without the uncertainty of independent fundraising.

Impact

What that actually changes on the ground.

Fewer manual workflows

Field and back-office processes that used to run on spreadsheets and phone calls, automated end to end.

Knowledge that stays

Operational knowledge captured and shared, instead of leaving the company when an engineer does.

Real-time visibility

Production data operators can actually see and act on, not just report on after the fact.
